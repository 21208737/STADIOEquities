## SS3 Part C: Overview

This folder contains the video presentation and supporting PowerPoint slides for Part C of the STADIOEquities data science project.

The presentation summarises the business problem of client non-activation and explains how data science can help STADIOEquities identify registered clients who may be unlikely to fund their accounts within the first six months.

The project applies a Random Forest classification model to client data to predict activation outcomes. The presentation discusses the data preparation workflow, model evaluation, key performance metrics, business implications, and recommendations for improving client activation.

## Presentation Objectives

The presentation explains the business problem and its impact on client acquisition spending. It also describe the data science approach and Random Forest modelling process. It presents and interprets the model's evaluation results and visualisations. Furthermore, it communicates findings to both business stakeholders and data science experts. It then recommends further validation and practical interventions to improve client activation.
